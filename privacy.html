Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

"Inner Shift tools" is a private desktop utility used only by the owner of the Inner Shift channels (innershift.somatic@gmail.com) to publish their own videos and read the statistics of their own accounts on TikTok and YouTube.

What data is used

Basic profile information, account statistics (followers, likes, video count) and the statistics of the owner's own videos, obtained through the official TikTok and YouTube APIs with the owner's permission.

Where it is stored

Only on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are kept in a local folder readable only by the owner. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other users

The tool does not collect data about other TikTok or YouTube users.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings and delete the local files.
